Kiara Lifestyle Clinic

Delhi, India
Wellness Clinic · Interiors

A lifestyle clinic conceived as a retreat — warm oak, travertine and woven textures across multiple floors, where wellness begins the moment you arrive.

Kiara reimagines the clinic as a place to slow down. Honey-toned oak wraps walls, joinery and a sculptural central staircase, grounded by pale travertine floors and soft, diffused light. The material palette is deliberately tactile — bouclé, cane and handwoven seats, dried botanicals and ceramic objects — so the space feels residential and restorative rather than clinical.

Public spaces flow openly: a branded reception leads into lounges screened by woven partitions, while a glass-walled consultation room keeps daylight and sightlines intact. Curved forms recur throughout — rounded sofa backs, arched mirrors and the gentle sweep of the stair — softening every transition through the building.

Quiet wayfinding names the treatment suites — Bloom, Zen — carrying the brand through warm oak corridors lit to feel calm rather than corporate. Restrained accents of terracotta and clay warm the neutral base, completing a clinic that reads as an unhurried, considered escape.
